# P3 summary: trace sim on Criteo DAC, 2M and 10M rows

Source: first N rows of Criteo `train.txt` (P2), 26 categorical tables, one cache per table,
capacity in vectors (64 .. 100,000). Policies: LRU, LFU (persistent counters), LFU (gem5 LFURP,
reset on eviction), TinyLFU-exact, W-TinyLFU (Count-Min), ARC, Belady (offline bound).
Jobs: `results/P3/jobs.txt` (2M: 412934-412936, 10M: 412937-412939; all tasks COMPLETED 0:0).
Full tables: `results/P3/TABLES.md`, generated by `tools/p3_tables.py` from these files only:
`results/P3/rows_{2000000,10000000}/figs/hit_rates.csv` (every policy x capacity x table, plus "all")
and `results/P3/rows_{2000000,10000000}/figs/zipf_per_table.csv`.

## Does LFU beat LRU at 256 vectors?
Yes, at both 2M and 10M, for both LFU variants (all tables combined). The margin is not stable:
persistent-counter LFU's lead shrinks from 2M to 10M, and it loses on 6 tables at 10M.

| 256 vectors, all tables | 2M hit % | 10M hit % |
|---|---:|---:|
| LRU | 67.3151 | 67.0796 |
| LFU (persistent counters) | 71.8034 | 68.3280 |
| LFU (gem5 LFURP, reset on eviction) | 70.0951 | 70.3276 |
| W-TinyLFU (Count-Min) | 73.2679 | 73.1009 |
| ARC | 72.6030 | 72.4225 |
| Belady (offline bound) | 78.6721 | 78.4180 |

Per table at 256 vectors (hits compared exactly, `tools/p3_tables.py` output):
- 2M: persistent LFU > LRU on 18 tables, tie on 8, worse on 0; LFURP > LRU on 17, tie 8, worse on 1 (table 1).
- 10M: persistent LFU > LRU on 12, tie 8, worse on 6 (tables 1, 4, 10, 12, 14, 17);
  LFURP > LRU on 16, tie 8, worse on 2 (tables 1, 4).
- Across the whole sweep (64 .. 100,000), both LFU variants beat LRU combined at every capacity, at both 2M and 10M.
- TinyLFU-exact is below LRU at 256 for 10M (64.5267 vs 67.0796) but above it at 2M (69.7367).

## Zipf exponents (rank-frequency fit, counts >= 5, needs >= 10 ranks)
- 24 of 26 tables fitted at both sizes. Tables 8 and 19 have 3 unique ids: no fit.
- 2M: alpha from 1.0733 (table 2) to 4.9724 (table 5). 10M: 1.0759 (table 2) to 5.6033 (table 5).
- Per-table values: `TABLES.md` (from `zipf_per_table.csv`).

## Caveats
- Capacity is in vectors; no byte size is stated in these results, so no KB figure is given here.
- This is a trace simulation (CPU), not gem5 and not a GPU measurement.
