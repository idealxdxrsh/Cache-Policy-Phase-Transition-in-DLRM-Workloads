# P3b: shared-cache trace simulator, real Criteo 2M and 10M rows

Results: `RESULTS.md` (generated by `tools/p3b_results.py` from `rows_{2000000,10000000}/parts/*.csv` + JSON).
Jobs (`jobs.txt`): 2M 413067, 10M 413106, both COMPLETED 0:0 on `compute`, 4 CPUs. No GPU.

## Model (`trace_sim/run_shared_cache.py`)
- ONE cache shared by all 26 tables (P3 used 26 private caches, see `results/P3/ANALYSIS.md`).
- Order: Criteo rows in file order; within a row, tables 0..25. Empty/non-hex fields give no access,
  same rule as P3; per-table access counts equal P3's at 2M and 10M (checked).
- Key = table << 32 | id. Policies imported unchanged from run_trace_sim.py: LRU, LFU reset-on-eviction
  (gem5 LFURP), LFU persistent counters, ARC, W-TinyLFU. Capacities 256, 1000, 10000, 100000 vectors total.
- Each (policy, capacity) is written to `parts/*.csv` as soon as it finishes; parts equal the JSON at both sizes.

## Tests (synthetic data only)
- `tests/test_shared_cache.py` 16 tests, suite 56 OK (`unittest.txt`); mutation 10/10 (`mutation.txt`), old check 16/16.

## Findings (`RESULTS.md`)
| 256 vectors, total hit % | 2M | 10M |
|---|---:|---:|
| LRU | 41.4010 | 41.2640 |
| LFU reset (LFURP) | 47.6719 | 48.1592 |
| LFU persistent | 41.0006 | 38.4896 |
| ARC | 50.9225 | 50.8773 |
| W-TinyLFU | 50.9749 | 50.9101 |
- LFURP beats LRU at every capacity at both sizes. Persistent LFU does NOT beat LRU at 256 (2M, 10M) or at
  1,000 at 10M; it beats LRU at 10,000 and 100,000 at both sizes.
- Persistent LFU is the best policy at 10,000 and 100,000 at 2M, but at 10M only at 100,000
  (10,000 at 10M: 78.3167 vs W-TinyLFU 80.6457). It loses the most from 2M to 10M (-2.5109 to -3.6112 pp
  at 256..10,000); the other policies move less than 0.7 pp.
- Five large tables (2M only): 20.9254% of accesses but 2.4969..4.6272% of hits at 256 vectors.
