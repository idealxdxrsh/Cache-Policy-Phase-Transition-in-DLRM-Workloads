## 3. Why: the flip-flops' clock-pin internal power in OpenSTA depends on how many slots are rewritten

The DFFR_X1 CK-pin internal-power tables are indexed by `when` states over D, Q, QN and RN; the per-cycle energy is 6.824-7.432 fJ in every RN = 1 state (rise + fall at clock slew 0; liberty file, extracted by `tools/p8_breakdown.py` ck_tables). OpenSTA weights each state by the product of the pins' own duties (`evalBddDuty`, power/Power.cc), treating Q and QN as independent. A flip-flop that holds its value for the whole window gets the full energy; one whose Q has duty p in the window gets about (p^2 + (1-p)^2) of it, so one write in mid-window halves it, although the clock-pin energy of the real cell does not depend on that. The model below reproduces OpenSTA: on every flip-flop with no D or Q toggle in the window, the difference from OpenSTA's per-instance internal power is at most 2.6e-07 (relative); with all data activity 0 it gives the clock-only OpenSTA result (`results/P8/power/d16_m*_clockonly.txt`).

"Exact state" weights each state by the fraction of the window the flip-flop actually spent in that (D, Q) state (QN = not Q, RN = 1); it is recomputed by `tools/p8_breakdown.py` from the liberty tables and the VCD, not an OpenSTA output.

Findings: of the seed-1 gap of 17.743 pJ, 17.854 pJ is the CK-pin internal term of the OpenSTA model. The two modes toggle about the same number of data bits in the window, but mode 0 writes the fills in the window into 1 data_store slot(s) and mode 1 into 47, so far more flip-flops in mode 1 change state once or a few times and lose part of their clock-pin energy in the OpenSTA model. With exact-state weighting the CK-pin energy of the two modes is equal to within 0.001 pJ, and mode 0 minus mode 1 becomes -0.111, -0.069, -0.032 pJ (seeds 1, 2, 3). **The 76.33 vs 58.59 pJ difference is explained, and it is an artifact of the OpenSTA state-probability model combined with how many slots the window rewrites, not a difference in switching activity or in real clock energy; it should not be reported as a mode-0 vs mode-1 energy difference.** The same model also lowers every vector-based Sequential internal figure in `results/P8/RESULTS.md` by an amount that depends on the window (column "CK-pin pJ" OpenSTA model vs exact state).
