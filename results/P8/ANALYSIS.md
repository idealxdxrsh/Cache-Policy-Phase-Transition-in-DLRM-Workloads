The mode gap in OpenSTA's dynamic energy reported earlier (commit 56a0aa6) sits in row (c), OpenSTA's weighting of the clock-pin term (beyond the spread: yes). With the recalculated clock-pin energy (rows a, b) the modes do not differ beyond the seed spread. Why (c) differs: mode 0 wrote the window's fills into 1 data_store slot(s), mode 1 into 47 (seed 1); data flip-flops with Q changed in the window 124 vs 5743, data Q toggles 25813 vs 25451. OpenSTA gives a flip-flop whose Q has duty p in the window about (p^2 + (1-p)^2) of the clock-pin energy, so spreading the same number of writes over more flip-flops lowers (c) without any change in real clock energy. Rows marked "yes" other than (c), if any, are measured differences beyond the seed spread for this stimulus and window only; each is weighed below against the bias the section-3 test measured for that component.

- (e) combinational internal pJ (OpenSTA): mode 0 - mode 1 is 2.28-4.39% of the mode-0 value. The two modes are different netlists (37,939 vs 38,692 cells, seed-1 breakdowns), so a component can differ through the synthesized logic as well as through activity. The section-3 test (one other netlist and window) found OpenSTA's weighting moving this component by 2.23%, against a smallest mode difference of 2.28%; these are comparable, so the mode difference in this component is not established beyond the possible bias.
- (g) leakage mW (OpenSTA): mode 0 - mode 1 is 1.23-1.28% of the mode-0 value. The two modes are different netlists (37,939 vs 38,692 cells, seed-1 breakdowns), so a component can differ through the synthesized logic as well as through activity. The section-3 test found OpenSTA's weighting moving leakage by at most 0.10%, under half of the mode difference. The difference is consistent with the two netlists differing in cells; that cause was not tested separately.

## 2. Clock-pin recalculation and its cross-checks

Per flip-flop and clock cycle, the CK-pin energy is rise + fall of the liberty CK internal_power table of the state the cell is in (`when` over D, Q, QN, RN), at clock slew 0 (ideal clock; linear extrapolation through the first two table points, as OpenSTA's Table1 lookup does). Recalculation: states weighted by the time each flip-flop spent in them in the window (QN = not Q, RN = 1). Energies per state, fJ:

- DFFR_X1: !D & !RN & !Q & QN 7.1644; !D & RN & !Q & QN 7.1687; !D & RN & Q & !QN 7.4315; D & !RN & !Q & QN 22.2562; D & RN & !Q & QN 6.8244; D & RN & Q & !QN 7.1253
- DFF_X1: !D & !Q & QN 6.9557; !D & Q & !QN 7.5913; D & !Q & QN 7.5515; D & Q & !QN 6.9078

**Cross-check A, clock-only OpenSTA run** (`synthesis/p8/run_p8.sh clockonly`: all data pins activity 0, duty 0.5; CK 2 transitions per cycle; RN duty 1). The same tables with OpenSTA's independent weighting at those duties must give OpenSTA's Sequential internal power:

**Cross-check B, OpenSTA per instance**: on every flip-flop with no D or Q toggle in the window, OpenSTA's internal power is its CK-pin term alone; the tables with OpenSTA's weighting match it within 2.6e-07 (relative) over 315,017 flip-flop-windows in 22 runs.

**Cross-check C, per-event count** (`d16_m0_random_gl_w20000_n10000`): counting each clock edge in the state after the time step gives 10.5602 mW; the time-weighted recalculation gives 10.5713 mW (+0.11%); OpenSTA's weighting gives 9.9461 mW (debug sum, 3 significant digits).

## 3. Does the state-probability weighting affect the other components? Test on one netlist

Run `d16_m0_random_gl_w20000_n10000` (`results/P8/bias/d16_m0_random_gl_w20000_n10000.json`), window 40005000-60004999 ps, 11,466 cells, 0 unmapped pins. Method in `tools/p8_bias_check.py`: OpenSTA's own per-arc energies (debug log) are kept and only its probability weights are replaced by counts from the activity file. Simultaneous changes and `when` states are taken after the time step (zero delay).

| component | OpenSTA mW | recomputed mW | difference | can the weighting affect it? |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| flip-flop CK pin | 9.94609 | 10.56022 | +6.17% | yes (Q, QN independent); recalculated |
| flip-flop D pin | 0.02041 | 0.03229 | +58.24% | yes (CK state at the D change; zero-delay timing decides it); small next to the CK pin |
| flip-flop Q/QN outputs | 0.15439 | 0.15448 | +0.06% | only through the split between arcs; no material effect here |
| combinational internal (outputs) | 0.47269 | 0.46217 | -2.23% | only through the split between arcs; 930 output toggles had no input change in the same step and kept OpenSTA's weights |
| combinational switching | 0.68910 | 0.68910 | +0.00% | no: 0.5 C V^2 x toggle rate; OpenSTA's toggle rate equals the activity file's within 0.50% (3-digit rounding) on all 4859 output pins |
| flip-flop leakage | 0.26125 | 0.26150 | +0.10% | yes in principle (state-weighted); negligible here |
| combinational leakage | 0.23250 | 0.23236 | -0.06% | yes in principle (input states taken as independent); negligible here |

Combinational input pins have no internal-power groups in this netlist's cells (none in the debug log). The test covers one netlist and one window; other windows were not tested.
