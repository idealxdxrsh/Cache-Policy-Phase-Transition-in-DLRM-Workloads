# P8 results: power of rtl-v2-final (P5e-flow 500 MHz netlists) at 500 MHz

500 MHz netlists (P5e flow). Depths 16 and 64 meet 500 MHz; depth 256 misses it by 0.0357 ns (mode 0) and 0.0015 ns (mode 1), inside the flow noise, so 500 MHz at depth 256 is not demonstrated (results/P5e/SUMMARY.md).

Fill data: random ($random, seed 1); real embedding vectors are not random, so data-path switching here is not that of real embeddings.

Clock group = 0 W: the clock is ideal (no clock-tree cells); the flip-flops' clock-pin internal power is in Sequential. Power in mW from OpenSTA `report_power` (`synthesis/p8/power.tcl`); dynamic energy per cycle = (internal + switching) x 2 ns. Generated by `tools/p8_results.py`.

## (b) Vector-based (zero-delay gate-level simulation, VCD of the recorded window)

Label: NanGate45, typical corner, pre-layout, no clock tree, no extracted parasitics, flip-flop storage, activity from zero-delay gate-level simulation (Icarus, Yosys-generated cell models from the liberty file), VCD of the recorded window over 10,000 cycles

Zero delay: no glitches, so combinational switching and internal power are lower bounds for this activity. Hit rate = hits / responses in the recorded window; a slice's window hit rate is not a steady-state value.

**Caveat (results/P8/ANALYSIS.md):** OpenSTA weights the flip-flops' clock-pin internal power by state probabilities that treat Q and QN as independent, so Sequential internal power here falls for every flip-flop that changes state in the window. The depth-64 mode-0 vs mode-1 gap (one slot rewritten vs 47) is that artifact, not a mode effect; ANALYSIS.md gives exact-state recomputed values.
