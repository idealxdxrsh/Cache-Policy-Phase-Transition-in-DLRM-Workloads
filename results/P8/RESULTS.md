500 MHz netlists (P5e flow). Depths 16 and 64 meet 500 MHz; depth 256 misses it by 0.0357 ns (mode 0) and 0.0015 ns (mode 1), inside the flow noise, so 500 MHz at depth 256 is not demonstrated (results/P5e/SUMMARY.md).

Label of every power and energy number: "NanGate45, typical corner, pre-layout, no clock tree, flip-flop storage, random fill data, activity over <N> cycles from <slice>"; N and the slice are given per row. Fill data is random ($random); real embedding vectors are not random, so data-path switching here is not that of real embeddings. No clock-tree cells (ideal clock): the clock-tree power of a layout is not included. Zero-delay simulation: no glitches. Generated by `tools/p8_results.py` from the files named in its header; the tests behind the component choices are in `results/P8/ANALYSIS.md`.

### Can OpenSTA's state-probability weighting affect each component? (test on `d16_m0_random_gl_w20000_n10000`, `results/P8/bias/d16_m0_random_gl_w20000_n10000.json`)

| component | how OpenSTA weights it | test: OpenSTA vs recomputed from the activity file | verdict |
|---|---|---|---|
| clock-pin internal (a, b) | product of D, Q, QN, RN duties for each `when` state | per-event recomputation 10.5602 mW vs OpenSTA 9.9461 mW (+6.17%) | biased: replaced by the recalculation |
| flip-flop other internal (d): D pin | `when` duties (CK, Q, QN, RN) | 0.0204 vs recomputed 0.0323 mW | affected, but small: under 0.3% of the clock-pin term; zero-delay timing decides the CK state at a D change |
| flip-flop other internal (d): Q, QN outputs | output toggle rate x arc energies; only the split between arcs uses duties | 0.1544 vs 0.1545 mW (+0.06%) | not materially affected |
| combinational internal (e) | output toggle rate x arc energies; split between arcs from independent sensitization duties | 0.4727 vs 0.4622 mW (-2.23%; 930 output toggles kept OpenSTA weights) | affected by a few percent at most in this test |
| switching (f) | 0.5 C V^2 x toggle rate; no duty term | OpenSTA density equals the activity file's toggle rate within 0.50% (3-digit debug rounding) on 4859 output pins, none beyond 1% | not affected |
| leakage (g) | per-state leakage x independent duties, remainder at the cell's average leakage | flip-flops 0.26125 vs 0.26150 mW (+0.10%); combinational 0.23250 vs 0.23236 mW (-0.06%) | affected in principle, negligible in this test |

## 3. Criteo slices: position, requests, hit rate, and trace-simulator hit counts on the same requests

- `row0`: starts at row 0 (access 0 of the 2M-row shared-cache stream); 200,000 accesses in the slice file, 39,367 distinct keys, numbered in order of first appearance (`results/P8/stimulus/row0.json`).
- `row1M`: starts at row 1,000,000 (access 22,988,023 of the 2M-row shared-cache stream); 200,000 accesses in the slice file, 38,707 distinct keys, numbered in order of first appearance (`results/P8/stimulus/row1M.json`).
- Stream totals: 46,128,690 accesses, 2,293,344 distinct keys in 2,000,000 rows; P3b: 46,128,690 accesses and 2,293,344 keys (match; `results/P8/stimulus/stream.json`, `results/P3b/rows_2000000/shared_cache_results.json` meta).

Each run replays the slice from its start (cold cache) until the cycle budget ends; R = requests completed. Window = requests issued in the recorded cycles (indices first-last). Hit rate = hits / requests. Trace simulator (`trace_sim/run_trace_sim.py` LFUResetCache and LRUCache, unchanged) at 256 entries, cold start, on exactly the first R requests; its hits are counted over all R and over the window's requests. Known RTL differences: on equal counts the RTL evicts the lowest slot index, the simulator the entry that entered earliest; RTL counters saturate at 63, the simulator's at 255; RTL mode 1 keeps the slot's count for a new tag. Reference: P3b shared cache, LFU reset on eviction, 256 entries, whole 2M-row stream: 47.67% (`results/P3b/rows_2000000/shared_cache_results.json`). A slice's hit rate is not a steady-state value.
