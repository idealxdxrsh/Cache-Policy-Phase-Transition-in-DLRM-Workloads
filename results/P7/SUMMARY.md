## Findings worth reading before any write-up (details and paths in CLAIMS.md)
- **TSMC 28nm figures** (6,791 um2, 2.45 mW, 1,070 ps, 935 MHz, -65 ps): no source file; no power step was ever run.
  4,048 cells and 2,730 flip-flops are contradicted by the Yosys rerun (11,331 cells, 2,705 flip-flops).
- **83.74% / 5.15x**: a ratio of two annotated profiler ranges, not stalls. The printed values give 83.75%.
  **+3.00%** uses that ratio as f. With the 71.84% kernel share the Amdahl projection is +2.56% (README: +2.6%).
- **gem5:** only the 2 MB stats are committed; 256 kB and 512 kB have none. The LFU file has no IPC and no simTicks, so
  "+0.08% IPC" (README) / "+0.11%" (paper Fig. 4) and "300M ticks" have no source.
- **Counters:** "persistent saturating counters" is not in any RTL (no per-address history). The gem5 "LFU" is gem5's
  LFURP, so the 10,595 fewer misses are not the RTL's.
- **Cause of the small-cache penalty:** in trace simulation, reset-on-eviction LFU beats LRU everywhere tested. Only
  persistent LFU in the shared cache loses at 256 vectors. "Phase transition" is therefore marked UNSUPPORTED.
- **Waveform:** the VCD has freq_count[13] = 23, [15] = 13, [14] = 7. The paper's "lines 14 and 15 reach 23 and 13" is wrong.
- **Dataset and fit:** the data is Criteo DAC, not Terabyte. The Zipf fits give alpha 1.0733-5.6033, not ~0.85.
