(`<run>/sta.txt`, worst max path, 1 GHz netlists). Register names come from the netlists, which are not committed.
Reading (not a tool output): at depths 64 and 256 every listed path starts at a Stage A register (`stageA_min`),
so it runs through the Stage B leaf lookup and tree (`victim_comb`, EVICT cycle), then a slot decode. It ends at a
`valid_bits` D pin (the EVICT clear, RTL line 286) or at a `data_store` D pin. data_store is written only in FILL
from `victim_reg` (line 295), so the second kind probably goes through a write-select structure that synthesis
shares between the EVICT and FILL writes. STA treats it as a real path; whether any cycle can exercise it was
not checked, and it is not declared false. The hit path (address -> `freq_cnt`) is no longer the worst path.
The flat-vector attempt's paths are in `attempt1_packed_vectors/CRITICAL_PATH.md`.
