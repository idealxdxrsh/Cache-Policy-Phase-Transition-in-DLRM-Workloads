\section{Where the project stands}
The trace study and the controller are complete up to a routed layout; result numbers below are those of the paper
draft (\path{paper/v2}), generated from \path{results/}.
\begin{itemize}
\item \textbf{Finding.} In one cache shared by all Criteo tables, the exact model of the LFU controller with
lowest-index ties (v2) loses to LRU at every size simulated from \R{sz.256} to \R{sz.100000} entries:
\R{p9.10M.256.v2}\% against \R{p3b.10M.256.LRU}\% at \R{sz.256} entries on the first \R{lab.10M} rows
(\path{results/P9}, \path{results/P3b}).
\item \textbf{Fix.} Rotate-victim (v3) restores oldest-first LFU: \R{p11.10M.256.v3}\% at \R{sz.256} entries,
\R{d.v3lru} points above LRU (\path{results/P11}). RTL and simulator agree request by request on test slices
(\path{results/P10}).
\item \textbf{Hardware (STMicroelectronics 28nm FD-SOI, typical corner).} At depth \R{sz.256} both versions meet
\R{p15.met} ns and fail \R{p15.fail} ns after routing; in post-route simulation of the \R{p15.gls.period} ns layouts
with the testbench fill model, v3 needs \R{p15.dE.row1M.abs} to \R{p15.dE.row0.abs}\% less energy per request
(\path{results/P15}). Registers and the clock tree take all but \R{p15.logic.lo} to \R{p15.logic.hi}\% of the
power.
\item \textbf{GPU.} On one H100 the L2 read hit rate of the embedding kernels falls from \R{p14.pt.64}\% to
\R{p14.pt.16384}\% as the batch grows (\path{results/P14}); H200 counters are blocked by a permission error that
needs the cluster administrators (\path{results/P14/PREP.md}).
\end{itemize}
\textbf{Open, as stated in the results and in the paper's limitations.} Layouts were optimised at the typical corner
only; at the slow corner the derived periods are \R{p16.dp.v2.10} and \R{p16.dp.v3.10} ns and I/O hold fails because
the I/O constraints use an ideal external clock (\path{results/P16}). Every point is one run, so the v2/v3 timing
difference at \R{p15.fail} ns is not resolved. SDF simulations annotate \R{p15.sdf.check}\% of the timing checks.
Energy per request rests on a fill model of up to \R{p13.fill.max} random cycles, and activity covers
\R{p15.cov.v2} to \R{p15.cov.v3}\% of the driver nets. Hardware exists only up to \R{sz.256} entries, lines hold a
data word rather than a vector, and only one dataset and one trace order were studied.

\section{Goals, ranked}
Each goal has one measurable success criterion; ``v2/v3'' means both versions, mode 0, unless stated.

\begin{enumerate}[label=\textbf{\alph*.},leftmargin=1.6em]
\item \textbf{Multi-corner sign-off.} Write block-level I/O constraints with a clock latency per corner, set up
multi-mode multi-corner (MMMC) optimisation over the typical, slow and fast corners, and re-run place and route of
the depth-\R{sz.256} designs.
\emph{Success:} for v2 and v3, a met/failed clock-period bracket per corner with setup and hold met in all three
corners at the reported period, plus DRC and connectivity clean in the tool's checks.

\item \textbf{Clock gating and/or SRAM storage for the data array.} Add clock gating to the line registers (enable per
line on fill and counter update), and, if the design kit provides SRAM macros for this node (checked by file name
only), move the data array to SRAM.
\emph{Success:} power and energy per request before and after, same flow, same slices, same period, for v2 and v3;
the change reported with the activity-coverage bound.

\item \textbf{Set-associative controller with Rotate-victim per set.} Parameterise sets and ways, keep one
Rotate-victim pointer per set, extend the RTL-exact simulator to sets, and compare its hit rate with the
fully associative results on the same stream.
\emph{Success:} RTL that elaborates and passes the existing tests for capacities from 1,000 to 100,000 entries (or
the nearest sets$\times$ways configuration), request-by-request agreement with the simulator on test slices, and a
routed layout for at least the smallest of these sizes; the largest sizes in layout depend on goal~b.

\item \textbf{Realistic miss cost.} Put a cycle-level DRAM model (for example Ramulator or DRAMSim3) behind the cache
in place of the testbench fill model: replay the miss stream, take the per-miss latency from the DRAM model, and
re-run gate-level simulation and power with those latencies.
\emph{Success:} energy per request for v2 and v3 recomputed with the DRAM latencies, reported next to the current
fill-model numbers, with the DRAM model and its configuration named.

\item \textbf{Generality.} Repeat the tie-break study (LRU, oldest-first LFU-reset, v2 rule, v3 rule, ablation) on a
second dataset (for example Avazu) and with a second within-row access order on Criteo.
\emph{Success:} hit rates and the ablation for both, at the sizes of the current study, stating whether v2 still
loses to LRU and whether the tie-break still explains the whole gap.

\item \textbf{Run-to-run variation.} Repeat place and route of v2 and v3 at the failing and met periods with varied
utilisation and placement seeds.
\emph{Success:} the spread of setup WNS and area across runs per version, and a statement whether the v2/v3
difference exceeds it.

\item \textbf{SDF timing checks and H200 counters.} Find a write\_sdf and simulator setting that annotates the
conditional timing checks of the cell models; re-run the H200 counter job once the administrators grant access.
\emph{Success:} a non-zero share of timing checks annotated, with the violation count of the post-route
simulations; the P14 table repeated on the H200.

\item \textbf{Paper.} Incorporate Prof.\ Mane's comments, add the results of goals a to f that are ready, choose
the venue with him, submit, and revise.
\emph{Success:} submitted manuscript that passes the number and leak checks; revision done within the venue's
deadline.
\end{enumerate}

\section{Resources and dependencies}
\begin{itemize}
\item \textbf{C2 lab server.} Reachable only from the lab network, through the master ssh socket. Lingering is
enabled, so long Genus, Xcelium and Innovus jobs start as \texttt{systemd-run -{}-user} units and survive logout; at most
4 tool jobs at a time. Raw reports stay off the repository; only aggregate tables are committed after the leak check.
\item \textbf{Cadence licences.} Genus, Xcelium and Innovus from the campus licence; the plan assumes 4
concurrent jobs and schedules C2 work in blocks of that size.
\item \textbf{Sharanga HPC.} Batch jobs only (\texttt{compute}, \texttt{gpu\_h100\_4}, and the H200 partition found
with \texttt{sinfo}). Scratch is purged without notice: the environment is pinned in
\path{cluster/dlrm_cache.explicit.txt} and rebuilt offline with \path{cluster/jobs/env_rebuild.sbatch}; every
job starts with an environment check, and results are copied back and committed after each phase.
\item \textbf{Prof.\ Mane.} Review of the paper draft (week 11), approval of the venue and of any newly published
aggregate results.
\item \textbf{Cluster administrators.} H200 performance-counter access (goal g).
\end{itemize}
